# Time Blocker
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-In_Development-orange) [![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-View_Site-2ea44f)](https://usetimeblocker.vercel.app/)

> **"Give every minute a job."** - Cal Newport

Time Block Planner is a minimalist digital adaptation of Cal Newport’s time-blocking methodology. It transforms reactive busyness into intentional deep work by separating task capture from daily scheduling.

Its unique multi-column adaptive grid mimics the flexibility of pen and paper, allowing you to visually "cross off" and rewrite disrupted plans without erasing your daily history.

### 📸 The Philosophy
This project was built to solve the frustration with standard digital calendars that are too rigid for reality. It is based on two core visual principles:

1. **Separation of Concerns (The Template)**: Unlike apps that mix your to-do list with your calendar, we enforce a strict separation.

   - The Collection Page (Left): A dedicated "messy" zone for capturing tasks, notes, and metrics.
   - The Time Block Grid (Right): A clean, intentional space for scheduling.

2. **The Reality of Disruption (The Adaptive Grid)**: Plans change. Standard apps force you to delete events, erasing the history of your day.


   - The Problem: A real day involves crossing out a failed plan and rewriting it.

   - The Solution: Our Adaptive Grid allows you to "cross off" the rest of the day and open a fresh column side-by-side to re-plan, preserving the history of your adaptations.


### ✨ Inspiration

This project is directly inspired by **Cal Newport's** physical Time-Block Planner and his underlying philosophy of time management, which he has advocated for over fifteen years.

The core idea is to create the definitive digital version of Newport's system, which emphasizes replacing stressful busyness with empowering intention. The application is built for users who want to practice "Deep Work" and apply principles of digital minimalism to their daily planning.
